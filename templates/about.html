Our Identity

Who We Are

A team of practitioners, educators, and builders — united by one goal.

{{ site_config.school_name|default:"ABrayTech Limited" }} is a UK & Nigeria-based technology training and digital transformation company, committed to closing the skills gap across Africa and the diaspora.

We deliver career-focused, practical training in AI, cybersecurity, full-stack development, data science, and digital marketing — built for the real world, not just the classroom.

Our programmes are crafted by industry practitioners and structured to take complete beginners through to job-ready professionals, with ongoing mentorship and community support.
