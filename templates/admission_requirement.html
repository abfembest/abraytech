How admission works

Four steps from application to your first lesson.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Choose a programme and fill out the application form.

  2. 2

    Pay and submit

    Pay the application fee securely online and upload your documents.

  3. 3

    Review

    Our admissions team reviews complete applications as they come in.

  4. 4

    Start learning

    Accept your offer in the student portal and open your courses.

If you're accepted

You'll get your decision by email and in the student portal. Accept the offer to unlock your programme's courses.

If we need more information

We'll email you exactly what's missing so you can complete your application.

Before you apply

A few practical things to have ready before you start your application.

Technical setup

  • A computer (laptop or desktop)
  • A reliable internet connection
  • Time to commit to self-paced coursework

We ask about your setup on the application form. It doesn't affect your admission decision.

Documents to submit

  • Completed application form
  • A valid ID document
  • Certificate or transcript of your highest qualification
  • Application fee payment
  • Optional: personal statement, resume or CV
