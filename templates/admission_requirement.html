Admission Requirements

Meet these criteria to be eligible for admission to our programs

Who Can Apply

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • No prior coding or technical experience required
  • No entrance exam, GPA, or standardized test scores needed
  • Career changers, students, and working professionals all welcome

Some programs list additional recommended background on their program page.

What You'll Need

  • A computer (laptop or desktop)
  • A reliable internet connection
  • Time to commit to self-paced coursework

We ask about your setup on the application form — it doesn't affect your admission decision.
